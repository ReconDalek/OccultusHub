The Pact

An 18-night contract with the dark — a business simulation re-bound as occult ritual, for the Order.

Design plan  ·  v0.6  ·  occultusHub / Games

I

The Premise

A group convenes and each runs a fledgling cabal — solo, or as a team that votes on its moves. Over 18 nights everyone is handed the same crossroads, and each cabal chooses its own answer. The night turns only once every cabal has committed. Feed the pact and it grants Dominion; overreach and your coffers run dry.

What differs is the reading: which risks you take, what you spend, what you let wither. After the twelfth night the veil thins and Fate's Dice enters every outcome — the same choice can bless or ruin you.

Run Gold below zero and the Pact is Broken — the run ends where it stands, however much Dominion you'd gathered. And Dominion means little without Thralls to hold it: a following you neglect costs you most of your favour at the Reckoning.

Vocabulary — "Dark Pact" set Customers → Thralls  •  Inventory → Offerings  •  Profit → Dominion  •  Cash → Gold  •  Bankruptcy → The Pact is Broken  •  post-night-12 randomiser → Fate's Dice
II

The Four Resources

☩
Gold
start 1,000 · no cap
Scarce and precious. Many choices cost Gold; income is rare and always paid for with something else. Drop below 0 on any night → the Pact is Broken, run over. Gold is a "spend or don't" pressure and a bankruptcy line — it barely touches the score (a small hoarding bonus, that's all).
⛧
Offerings
start 0 · cap 90 · not scored
Ritual fuel — burnt to produce Dominion. Not part of the score. The vault holds 90; a choice that would push past it fills to 90, spoils the excess, and inflicts the overfill penalty: −80 Dominion, −3 Thralls. Empty when a ritual needs it → the ritual fails.
✦
Dominion
start 0 · no cap · the headline
The pact's favour — the number every cabal chases and the leaderboard is built on. Grown all game, never spent, never protects you from ruin. Bad omens claw it back; it floors at 0.
☾
Thralls
start 0 · no cap · the gate
Bound followers. They scale your Dominion at the Reckoning — a cabal with no flock keeps ~30% of its favour; a large devoted following doubles it. Several "rite with the faithful" options also grant Dominion per Thrall, so a following actively earns favour, not just multiplies it.

The shape of it: chase Dominion, but you cannot cash it in without followers — and you cannot afford much of anything if you let Gold run dry. Offerings are the tactical supply that powers your biggest Dominion swings.

III

The Round

18 nights. Each night every cabal sees one scenario and four responses, A–D, each applying a set of resource changes — spend Gold for Dominion, burn Offerings, recruit or sacrifice Thralls. Most mix a give and a take. Everyone faces an identical script; the night turns once all cabals commit.

Nights 1–12 — The BargainOutcomes are fixed. Same choice → same result. Pure decision-making under a tight Gold constraint.
Nights 13–18 — The ThinningFate's Dice rolls on the chosen option — cost and reward together, worst to best.

Nothing is blocked. You keep your own books.

The game never greys out a choice, never warns you, never previews an outcome. Every option is always selectable. The screen keeps an honest tracker — current Gold / Offerings / Dominion / Thralls and a ledger of every past night — but the planning is yours: read the option, do the maths, decide whether you can carry it, before you commit.

MistakeWhat happens
Gold below 0, any nightThe Pact is Broken. Run ends where it stands. Score 0.
A choice would push Offerings past 90The rest of the option resolves; the vault fills to 90, the excess spoils, and you take −80 Dominion, −3 Thralls.
A choice costs more Offerings / Thralls than you holdThe ritual fails — any Gold it cost is lost, nothing else happens, the night is spent. (This is the anti-exploit rule: you can't "sacrifice" souls you don't have.)

Delayed payoffs ⏳

A few options pay — or bite — several nights later. The cost lands now; the ledger holds the pending line and resolves it on the named night. e.g. Night 3: "Seize a slice of the rival's standing" — Dominion +50 now, Gold +450 on night 6. Three in the first twelve nights, one in the dice nights. They must be planned around, not reacted to.

Rites with the faithful ✦

Four options across the game read "Dominion + (Thralls × N)" — they convert your current following straight into favour. A cabal that has built its flock gets an enormous swing here; a cabal that hasn't gets almost nothing. This is what makes followers worth protecting during the game, not just at the end.

Each night one option is a low-stakes hold — small, safe effects — but its position rotates (it is not always D), and it is a choice, not a lifeline: it won't dig you out of a hole.

IV

Fate's Dice — Nights 13–18

On nights 13–18, when a cabal commits, it casts Fate's Dice — a single d6, shown as a physical die-throw animation on screen. Each cabal rolls its own (a team rolls once for the team). The face that lands picks one band, applied to every value on the chosen option at once:

RollBand"Strike the rival coven first" (Night 14-A), resolved
1–2Ill FortuneGold −350 · Thralls −4 · Dominion +30  (a bloody repulse)
3–4The TurningGold −250 · Thralls −2 · Dominion +120  (a costly win)
5–6FavourGold −150 · Thralls 0 · Dominion +260  (a rout)

Every value is authored as a [low, mid, high] triple ordered worst → best for the player. Costs swing modestly; rewards swing wide — you can plan for the worst-case cost, but never the payoff. One roll, one band, across the whole row: a risky play fails on all fronts or wins on all fronts.

Archetypes, one spread per night: Safe (narrow, mildly positive in every band) · Balanced (Ill Fortune stings but survives, Favour is solid) · Gamble (wide upside, real cost to the other resources) · Hold (tiny, near-flat). Position rotates.

Determinism: the roll for cabal C on night R is seeded from (sessionId, cabalId, R) — reproducible and auditable server-side, different for every cabal.

V

The Score

Score = Dominion × loyaltyMod × cashMod   (× 10 for display)
loyaltyMod = clamp( 0.3 + Thralls / 25 , 0.3 , 2.0 )
cashMod    = clamp( 0.75 + Gold / 4000 , 0.75 , 1.5 )
  • Dominion is the thing being multiplied — the number every option chases and every cabal grows all game.
  • loyaltyMod — no followers → you keep just 30% of your Dominion at the Reckoning. A following of 25+ doubles it. This is "you need followers to your Dominion", made literal.
  • cashMod — minor. Starting Gold sits at ~1.0×; finishing with a treasury nudges toward 1.5×, near-broke down to 0.75×. Gold is mostly the bankruptcy line, not a score lever.
  • Offerings — not in the score at all. Fuel + the overfill penalty.
  • Pact Broken → Score 0. Recorded with "fell on Night N"; all broken runs tie at the bottom.
Worked example. Reckoning — Dominion 950, Thralls 12, Gold 600.
loyaltyMod = 0.3 + 12/25 = 0.78  ·  cashMod = 0.75 + 600/4000 = 0.9  →  950 × 0.78 × 0.9 × 10 = 6,670.
Same Dominion, no followers (Thralls 2): 950 × 0.38 × 0.9 × 10 = 3,250 — over half the score gone.

Leaderboards

  • Season board — the primary. The month's completed runs by final Score. Resets monthly with the new scenario set.
  • All-time board — each player's best single season Score ever, with "seasons played / survived" alongside. MAX(final_score) GROUP BY user_id.
VI

The Setting

You've inherited the Order — a cold chapel above a sealed crypt, and a pact with the thing behind the door. Over 18 nights you feed it, and it feeds you favour. Your rivals: a coven across the river, the town's bishop and his inquisitors, and whatever you keep feeding in the dark. No factions, no forums — a self-contained occult world.

Notation   G Gold · O Offerings · D Dominion · T Thralls  |  ✦ rite with the faithful (D + T×N)  |  ⏳ delayed  |  dice nights: [Ill Fortune, Turning, Favour]

VIII

Content & Engine

The 18 scenarios live in one versioned file (the sanctumData.js pattern). The backend is sole authority on effects — the client renders scenario text and option labels only, never outcomes.

// one option. scalar = fixed; [a,b,c] = dice-band triple, worst -> best.
{ label: "Rally the faithful", archetype: "engine",
  effects: { gold: [-100,-60,-20], dominion: [10,22,40], dominionPerThrall: [3,5,7] } }

{ label: "Seize their standing",
  effects: { dominion: 50 },
  delayed: { on: 6, effects: { gold: 450 } } }

pactEngine.resolveChoice(state, scenario, optionKey, roll) — one pure module — picks the band, applies deltas (dominionPerThrall × current Thralls), clamps Offerings/Thralls at 0, applies the overfill penalty past 90, checks Gold < 0 → Pact Broken, and queues any delayed block. The session controller and any future Discord client call nothing else.

IX

How a Game Runs

The Pact is always played as a convened group — never a private idle save. One member opens the lobby and sets the game up; others join by code. It borrows The Rite's session-and-polling shape (no websockets).

The host is simply whoever opened the lobby. They choose the settings and start the game — and that is the extent of their power. The host has no influence on play: they don't advance nights, don't break ties, don't see anything other players can't.

Solo modeEvery member runs their own cabal — sees only their own scenario, books, and outcome. The night turns once every player has committed.
Team modeMembers split into teams — up to 4 each, sizes can be uneven. A team shares one cabal. Each member votes; when all have voted the majority option commits. The night turns once every team has committed.

Team voting — no captain

  • A team can commit early once a member has a majority the outstanding votes can't overturn (e.g. 3 of 4).
  • Ties break by Fate — the session RNG picks among the tied options. No captain, no host, no deadlock.

Game settings (set in the lobby)

  • Mode — Solo or Teams.
  • Per-night timer — OFF (default), or 90 / 120 / 180 seconds. On expiry any cabal that hasn't committed auto-takes the night's hold option — "the night passes them by".

Leaving & rejoining

  • A disconnected player can rejoin the session and pick up exactly where their cabal stands.
  • If the group is only waiting on a missing cabal to advance, a 90-second countdown starts. Rejoin in time → play resumes. Miss it → that cabal gives up: marked abandoned, out of the game, no score posted. In team mode only that member is dropped; the team plays on (a team abandons only if every member gives up).
  • A group of one is legal — the night turns the moment you commit.
  • Standings stay hidden during play (you see only "4 / 6 cabals have committed"); the full ranking is revealed at the Reckoning.
  • Every cabal that reaches night 18 posts its final_score to the active season's board, regardless of mode.

Endpoints (auth-required, routes.js convention; engine authoritative)

  • POST /api/pact/session create (host sets mode, timer_seconds) · POST /api/pact/session/:code/join · POST /api/pact/session/:code/team form/join a team (≤ 4) · POST /api/pact/session/:code/start (host).
  • GET /api/pact/session/:code/state — your cabal's resources + ledger, the current night's scenario text and four option labels, rollsDice, commit progress (n/total), your team's current vote tally, any active rejoin countdown. No effect data, no previews.
  • POST /api/pact/session/:code/vote {option} — solo: locks your choice. Team: casts/changes your vote; the server auto-commits the cabal when the votes settle a majority (ties → session RNG picks).
  • On commit the server rolls Fate's Dice if night > 12 (seeded sha(sessionId, cabalId, night) → 1–6), resolves via pactEngine, returns the die face + resolved deltas, writes the ledger. When all live cabals are committed (or the timer expires → non-committers take the hold option) → advance the night; after night 18 → compute every final_score, set status = ended.
  • POST /api/pact/session/:code/rejoin — reattach to your cabal after a disconnect.
  • GET /api/pact/leaderboard?scope=season|alltime · POST /api/admin/pact/season / reset / practice (admin gate, mirrors /api/admin/sanctum/reset).

Frontend — /pact route, built mobile-first

  • New card in src/pages/Games.jsx → route /pact; components under src/components/Pact/. Lobby / play / reckoning screens, following the GameRoom component split.
  • The tracker. Compact resource strip — Gold / Offerings (n / 90) / Dominion / Thralls — sticky on mobile, side rail on wide screens. Expandable ledger below the scenario: every past night, the choice, the dice band, each delta. The game keeps honest books; it never does your forward planning.
  • Night flow: scenario card → four full-width stacked option cards (tap to select, confirm to commit — no hover-only affordances). Team mode shows live vote tallies; the cabal locks once the votes settle a majority.
  • The die throw (nights 13–18): on commit, a physical d6 tumbles and settles on the cabal's own face — one roll, rendered with CSS 3D transforms (or a short pre-rendered sprite sequence), settling on the server-returned value so it can't be re-rolled. The landed face maps to Ill Fortune / The Turning / Favour, then the outcome deltas count up on the tracker. Honours prefers-reduced-motion (snap to the face, skip the tumble). Then a "waiting on N cabals" gate, then the next night. Ends on the Reckoning: Dominion × loyaltyMod × cashMod broken out line by line, then both boards.
  • Reconnect straight from /state. Zero rules logic on the client — it renders text and animates a result the server already computed.
  • Touch targets ≥ 44px, single column ≤ 640px, one type/spacing scale across breakpoints. Test at 360 / 768 / desktop.

XIII

Design — Locked

  • Structure — 18 nights; 1–12 fixed, 13–18 Fate's Dice across the whole chosen option (costs swing modestly, rewards swing wide), triples worst → best. One d6 per cabal per night, shown as a die-throw animation.
  • Resources — Gold (start 1,000; scarce; < 0 = Pact Broken) · Offerings (start 0, hard cap 90, overfill = −80 D / −3 T; not scored) · Dominion (the headline; floors at 0) · Thralls (gate the score + power "rite with the faithful" options that grant D per Thrall).
  • Score — Dominion × loyaltyMod × cashMod (×10 for display). loyaltyMod = clamp(0.3 + Thralls/25, 0.3, 2.0) · cashMod = clamp(0.75 + Gold/4000, 0.75, 1.5). Validated by Monte-Carlo (8k runs/strategy): followers are the winning path, Dominion-hoarding keeps ~⅓ of its score, pattern/spam play bankrupts. All constants are v1 tuning targets.
  • Anti-exploit — a choice you can't cover in Offerings/Thralls fails (Gold lost, night spent). No "sacrifice" of resources you don't hold. 3 delayed payoffs in nights 1–12, 1 in 13–18.
  • Seasons — monthly, new 18-night set each. Season board + all-time board (best single season). One run per player per season.
  • Play — always a group session. Mode Solo or Teams (≤ 4, uneven ok). Night turns when every live cabal commits, or the timer (OFF default / 90 / 120 / 180s) expires and non-committers take the hold option.
  • Teams — no captain; vote, majority commits, ties broken by session RNG. Host = lobby creator, settings only, no gameplay influence.
  • Leaving — rejoin anytime; if the group waits only on you, a 90s countdown, then abandoned, no score (team drops just that member).
  • Practice mode — admins start solo is_practice = 1 runs (unlimited, same rules) for playtesting; excluded from every leaderboard.
  • No help — no greyed options, no warnings, no previews. On-screen tracker + ledger only. Hold option rotates position.
  • Surface — /pact route, mobile-first. Name The Pact · "nights" · "the Pact is Broken" · "Fate's Dice".

What's left before code: a live playtest of the Season 1 night set (§VII) to tune the constants — then build the engine.
